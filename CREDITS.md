# CREDITS & LICENSES

## Audio Assets (SFX & Background Music)

All sound effects and background music located in `app/src/main/res/raw/` were synthesized algorithmically and custom-authored for the "Voice Filter: Speak Challenge" application.

| Asset | File | Sample Rate / Format | License | Description / Attribution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Success Chime** | `res/raw/ding.wav` | 16,000 Hz, 16-bit Mono WAV | CC0 1.0 Universal / Apache 2.0 | Synthesized 2-tone melodic bell chime (C6-E6) with exponential decay. Author: NoiChuan Team. |
| **Error Buzzer** | `res/raw/buzz.wav` | 16,000 Hz, 16-bit Mono WAV | CC0 1.0 Universal / Apache 2.0 | Dual-frequency low saw/square wave tone (165Hz/247Hz). Author: NoiChuan Team. |
| **Game Over / Crash** | `res/raw/crash.wav` | 16,000 Hz, 16-bit Mono WAV | CC0 1.0 Universal / Apache 2.0 | Low impact pitch drop sweep with noise burst. Author: NoiChuan Team. |
| **Background Music (BGM)** | `res/raw/bgm.wav` | 16,000 Hz, 16-bit Mono WAV | CC0 1.0 Universal / Apache 2.0 | Light acoustic marimba melodic progression loop (C - G - Am - F) at ~110 BPM. Soft-gain mastering to avoid microphone bleed. Author: NoiChuan Team. |
